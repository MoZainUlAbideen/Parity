# WCAG source material

The files in `raw/`, and the knowledge base built from them in
`src/parity/kb/data/`, contain text from the Web Content Accessibility
Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 and its Understanding documents.

Sources:
- https://github.com/w3c/wcag (Understanding documents)
- https://github.com/w3c/wai-wcag-quickref (structured success criteria data)

These repositories are published by W3C under the W3C Software and Document
License: https://www.w3.org/copyright/software-license-2023/

Parity quotes this material to explain and cite accessibility requirements.
